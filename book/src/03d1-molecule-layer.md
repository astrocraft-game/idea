# Molecule Layer

The molecule layer (E1) is the first inward descent — where the player enters matter as buildable terrain.

## Primary purpose

This layer is entered through shrinker machines built in regions (P1) or stations (O2). At molecular scale, the game world shifts dramatically:

- biological systems become the terrain — viruses, bacteria, molecular structures
- gene editing and molecular construction become the core verbs
- the player builds stabilizer structures and biological tools
- molecular pathways replace belts and logistics links

The important feeling is that matter itself has become the factory floor.

## What changes at this scale

Unlike every previous layer, the molecule layer deals with living systems. The terrain is not inert rock or empty space — it is active, dynamic, and potentially hostile.

Key new elements:

- **Viruses and bacteria** as both threats and tools
- **Gene editing** as a construction mechanism
- **Molecular bonds** as structural building blocks
- **Biological containment** as a defense mechanic
- **Living system dynamics** that react to the player's interventions

## Desired feel

This layer should feel:

- precise
- alien
- alive
- intensely consequential

At planetary or orbital scale, a design flaw can waste throughput. At molecular scale, a design flaw can trigger a biological cascade or destabilize a repair sequence.

## Core loops

### Molecular construction loop

1. enter a molecular environment through a shrinker
2. scan for biological structures and flux contamination patterns
3. place molecular anchor structures
4. bridge them into a stabilizing pattern
5. verify that the pattern holds under biological stress

### Gene editing loop

1. identify a useful biological structure or organism
2. analyze its molecular components
3. edit gene sequences to modify behavior or create tools
4. deploy the modified organism as part of the industrial chain

### Repair loop

1. identify where molecular bonds are slipping due to flux
2. isolate the damaged molecular structure
3. rebuild a trustworthy structural pattern
4. connect it to larger stabilizer systems

## Design constraints

### Local readability still matters

Even though this layer is abstracted and unusual, it still needs the same clarity principles as factory play:

- visible flow
- visible stress
- visible bottlenecks
- visible failure propagation

### The player must still feel like a builder

This layer should not become a puzzle game detached from the rest of the campaign. The player still places, routes, reinforces, and scales.

## Relationship to macro play

The molecule layer should matter because it provides capabilities that macro industry alone cannot:

- biological tools and organisms that enhance planetary and orbital production
- molecular-level understanding of flux contamination
- prerequisite research and construction for the deeper quantum descent (E2)

Later, the player builds atomic shrinkers within E1 instances to descend to E2 — the quantum layer where flux is confronted at its deepest level.

## Multiple instances

E1 instances are independent. A shrinker in a P1 region creates one E1 instance. A shrinker in an O2 station creates another. Each has its own molecular environment, biological dynamics, and stabilizer network.

The player can have many E1 instances across many regions and stations. Managing which to invest in, which to develop, and which to leave dormant is a strategic decision.
