# Star Layer

The star layer (S1) is the solar system view — a star at center, orbiting bodies, asteroid belts, and the routes that connect them.

## Primary purpose

This layer is about managing the resources and logistics of one star system.

It includes:

- sending miners to mine asteroids and satellites
- sending space stations to orbit new planets for exploration
- route creation and traffic prioritization between orbits
- corridor defense against flux events
- choosing which systems to prioritize
- managing the shape of expansion within the star system

## Desired feel

This layer should feel:

- strategic
- high-consequence
- spatial but not abstract

The player should feel that every new route commits them to a maintenance burden and a defense burden, not just a convenience upgrade.

## Key questions

- which orbit is worth establishing next
- where should asteroid mining focus
- which planet is worth sending a station to
- how much redundancy should the route graph contain
- when is a long vulnerable route acceptable versus building a closer alternative

## Planet access from star layer

A planet is visible in its orbit, but can only be explored if there is a station in that orbit. The player must:

1. identify a planet worth exploring
2. dispatch or build a station in the planet's orbit
3. only then descend through O1 → P2

This creates meaningful strategic decisions — sending a station to a planet is expensive and slow, so the player must choose carefully.

## Star layer loops

### Expansion loop

1. identify a resource need that cannot be met locally
2. prospect distant orbits or asteroid belts
3. establish a route and a forward station
4. bring the new site into the supply network

### Mining loop

1. send miners to an asteroid field
2. establish a shuttle route for cargo return
3. increase throughput without collapsing reliability
4. integrate asteroid materials into the broader economy

### Colonization loop

1. identify a planet worth developing
2. send a station to its orbit
3. establish shuttle and elevator links
4. begin exploring the planet surface through the new orbit

## Flux and danger

The star layer is where strange physics should be most palpable in macro play.

This is the right layer for:

- flux crystal extraction from deep asteroid belts
- containment events
- precursor artifacts with unstable behavior
- damaged routes causing isolation
- high-value but dangerous opportunities

The player should never feel fully safe here, only increasingly capable.

## Relationship to sector layer

The star layer is one node in the sector. Everything the player builds here — stations, routes, mining operations — becomes an asset (or liability) visible from S2. A well-developed star system is a strategic anchor. A neglected one is a vulnerability.
